Every deal below was read at its store (or a deals source naming the store) on the check time shown. Sale price, usual price, discount and a plain verdict: is it really a deal?

How we check

  1. We read the price at the store itself, or in a deals source that names the store and the price.
  2. We record the sale price, the usual (list) price and the exact time the price was read — shown on every deal.
  3. We give a verdict: Real deal (a genuine low), Strong (a big honest discount), Routine (an ordinary sale) or Inflated (the “usual” price looks pumped up, or the cut is tiny).
  4. No deal is invented. If we could not verify a price, it is not listed.

Prices change fast on Black Friday — always confirm the price at the store before you buy. Store links open the store in a new tab; this site loads nothing from the network itself.
